About Shine Coaching Institute

Quality Education Made Truly Affordable

Core Curriculum & Special Electives

Subjects Offered (Classes 7 to 10)

Four core foundational subjects for all classes, plus flexible special options in Chemistry and Assamese for Class 9 and 10.

01

Mathematics

Core • Classes 7, 8, 9 & 10

Step-by-step mastery of formulas, proofs, and real-world mathematical applications without fear.

  • Arithmetic & Algebra Mastery
  • Geometry, Mensuration & Trigonometry
  • Board Exam Problem Solving & Theorems
  • Regular Practice & Speed Drills
02

General Science

Core • Physics & Biology

Connecting scientific principles to natural phenomena through clear visual reasoning and experiments.

  • Fundamental Physics Laws & Numericals
  • Living Systems & Biological Processes
  • Environmental Science & Ecosystems
  • Concept-Driven Visual Notes
03

English

Core • Classes 7, 8, 9 & 10

Building impeccable grammar accuracy, expressive writing skills, and articulate board literature analysis.

  • Comprehensive Grammar Rules & Editing
  • Board Literature Chapters & Poetry Analysis
  • Formal Essays, Letters & Reports
  • Reading Comprehension & Vocabulary
04

Social Science

Core • Classes 7, 8, 9 & 10

Making historical events, civic systems, geography, and economics insightful and easy to recall.

  • Chronological Historical Milestones
  • Physical Geography & Resource Mapping
  • Democratic Institutions & Governance
  • Economic Concepts & Case Studies
05

Chemistry & Assamese

Class 9 & 10 Special Options

Targeted elective options: in-depth Chemistry reactions or Assamese literature & grammar.

  • 🧪 Chemical Reactions, Formulas & Equations
  • 🧪 Acids, Bases, Carbon Compounds & Numericals
  • 📜 Assamese MIL Literature, Prose & Poetry
  • 📜 Assamese Byakoron (Grammar) & Essay Writing

Class 9 & 10 Flexible Elective Choices

In addition to our 4 core subjects (Maths, Science, Social Science & English), Class 9 and Class 10 students can freely choose Chemistry, Assamese, Both (Chemistry + Assamese), or None (Core only).
